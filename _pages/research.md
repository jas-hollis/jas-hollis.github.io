## Research Overview

I am interested in asking questions about convective storm dynamics, polar processes, and teleconnections. Broadly, I want to focus my research on studying how different aspects of the atmosphere-ocean system interact, the mechanisms that control them, their sensitivities to changing climate conditions, and the potential impacts these processes may have on global communities. The methods that I enjoy include working with observational and reanalysis datasets, analyzing climate model and estimate solution outputs, implementing numerical methods to study climate processes, and conducting data visualization and analysis in MATLAB and Python. I hope to use my skills and interests to contribute to meaningful work in climate solutions.
