## Research Overview

I am interested in asking questions about convective storm dynamics, polar processes, and teleconnections. Broadly, I want to focus my research on studying how different aspects of the atmosphere-ocean system interact, the mechanisms that control them, their sensitivities to changing climate conditions, and the potential impacts these processes may have on global communities.
